Typestrike

A typewriter for iPhone, iPad and Mac, built first for iPhone Duo.

Get help

Ask a question or report a problem by opening an issue at github.com/FayneTien/typestrike/issues. Say which device and system version you use, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

How do I restore Typestrike Pro on another device?

Open the ⋯ button above the paper, then Settings › Typestrike Pro › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In the Settings app, tap your name › Subscriptions › Typestrike Pro. On a Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings › Subscriptions. Pro stays on until the end of the year you paid for; everything you typed stays yours.

Where are my documents?

In the Document Box (the box button at the top left). They are kept only on your device, in the app's own storage, and included in your device's backups.

How do I type in other languages?

The globe key on the typewriter keys goes on to the next of the keyboard's languages, such as French, German, Pinyin, Zhuyin, Japanese or Korean. Hold it to choose a language, change the list or use the system keyboard.

Can I use a hardware keyboard?

Yes. Connect one to iPhone or iPad and the on-screen keys step aside; on a Mac, type on its own keyboard.
